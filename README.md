Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261122** — "Sum up 22
November's world news in one image" ($100, 50 places paid).

Running `main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes the entry
image to `/output/entry.png`. The image ships in this repo as
`entry.png`, drawn by `gen_image.py` (PIL, fixed text and layout, no
randomness): a newspaper front page for Sunday 22 November 2026.

**The day, in one image**

- **Lead — COP31 collapses in Antalya:** the UN climate summit ends
  with no deal after record overtime. The EU refuses a draft with no
  mention of fossil fuels; COP31 president Murat Kurum concedes there
  is no consensus. The longest COP ever, and the first major breakdown
  since Copenhagen in 2009.
- **Sea drones hit a warship:** suspected Ukrainian maritime drones
  strike a Russian naval vessel at Benghazi Naval Base, Libya.
- **Iran strikes Bahrain:** projectiles hit a civilian industrial
  facility in Sitra; a fire, two people injured.
- **Kyiv apartment hit:** a Russian drone strikes a five-storey block
  in Dymer; 14 missing. A ballistic missile on Kryvyi Rih kills a
  7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman; 60 wounded.
- **El Chapo Isidro caught:** Mexican special forces capture cartel
  boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in Sinaloa; 11 killed in the raid.
- **Also today:** a candy factory fire in Cikande, Indonesia, kills 3
  with 36+ missing; 90+ countries sign the electricity pledge; 47
  countries join the methane pledge.

The page also carries a day-in-Antalya timeline and an explicit date
check: every story happened or was first reported on 22 November 2026.

The image is 1024×1024 PNG, ~162 KB, and byte-identical on every run.
